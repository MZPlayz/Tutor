# Notification Lifecycle

1. **Booking Initialized:** Push Notification to Tutor.
2. **Payment Success:** SMS to Tutor + Student.
3. **T-Minus 60m:** SMS Reminder to Tutor (Landmark details).
4. **T-Minus 15m:** SMS Reminder to Student (Tutor is coming).
5. **Check-in:** Push Notification to Student: "Your tutor has arrived at the landmark."
6. **T-Plus 120m:** SMS to Student: "Rate your session."
