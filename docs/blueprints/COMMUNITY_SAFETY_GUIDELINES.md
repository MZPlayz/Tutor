# Community Safety Protocols

- **Home Tuition Safety:** First-time tutors are encouraged to bring a friend to the landmark for the first session.
- **Verified Badge:** Mandatory. No unverified person ever gets a home address.
- **Emergency Button:** 
    - "Help" button in active booking screen.
    - **On click:** Capture current GPS location (latitude, longitude).
    - **Action:** Send SMS to Admin with: "EMERGENCY: [User Name] needs help. Location: https://maps.google.com/?q=[lat],[lng] - Booking #[ID]"
    - Also trigger push notification to Admin dashboard.
- **Check-In Proof:** Tutor must click "I am at the landmark" in the app to establish `checkInTime`. Without this, tutor cannot contest any "No-Show" dispute.
